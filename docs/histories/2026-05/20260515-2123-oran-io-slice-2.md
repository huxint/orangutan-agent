### Design Intent

`oran-io` is the lowest-risk next foundation slice after `oran-async`: it exercises
the coroutine/executor surface without introducing SQLite state, provider protocols,
or tool permissions yet. The public surface is intentionally policy-free. Permission
evaluation and hook publication belong to higher layers that wrap these helpers, not
to the reusable file I/O primitive itself.

Public paths are UTF-8 strings rather than `std::filesystem::path` so `<filesystem>`
stays in `src/oran-io/file.cpp` instead of leaking through public headers. The
coroutine helpers check cancellation before dispatch and immediately before entering
the blocking standard-library operation; mid-system-call interruption is not provided
by the C++ standard library and is documented in `docs/design-docs/io-runtime.md`.
